The UTM gallery has been retired. Check out the [guides](https://docs.getutm.app/guides/guides/) for help creating a new virtual machine.

You may be interested in the [community run gallery](https://naveenrajm7.github.io/utm-gallery/). Virtual machines there are not made or reviewed by the UTM project, so download them at your own risk.

The old gallery images are still available, but they are no longer maintained:

- [GitHub releases](https://github.com/utmapp/vm-downloads/releases)
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/@osy86)
